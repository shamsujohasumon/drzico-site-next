Profile

Dr. Md. Isma Azam, known to patients as Dr. Zico, is a neurosurgeon in Dhaka who treats the full range of brain and spine conditions. His largest areas of work are lumbar disc (PLID) surgery, microscopic neck surgery (ACDF), brain tumors including endoscopic pituitary surgery, aneurysm surgery and hydrocephalus.

He is Associate Consultant, Neuro Surgery at Continental Hospital (formerly United Hospital), Gulshan 2, and previously served as Assistant Professor of Neurosurgery at Dhaka Medical College Hospital. His hospital lists more than 5,000 surgeries across his career; the case log below lists 5,010 by procedure.

He believes patients should understand their scans and their choices. Many people he sees do not need an operation, and he says so.

Technology and team

আধুনিক যন্ত্র ও দক্ষ টিম

Dr. Zico operates with the operating microscope, neuro-endoscope and EXOSCOPE, a high-definition 3D camera system for ultra-fine microsurgery. He uses keyhole and minimally invasive approaches when they are safe for the patient. Each operation involves a team of neurosurgery and anaesthesia consultants, specialists and trained OT nurses.
